# Where what is kept is discarded or rewritten

Every place `paulgsc/server` and `paulgsc/some-ui` delete or overwrite something they keep, with the
owner's ruling where one is written down. It is the starting inventory for `CLAUDE.md`'s
"Never lose the record": a site with no ruling is flagged, not judged. The owner's ruling on each
is #2265, and the stores themselves, with how each grows, are the `stores` of
`packages/assurance/src/ledger.json` (STORAGE3, #2225).

Deleting an account takes every row that account owns on home (`docs/identity.md`, invariants 9
and 12). That is listed once below rather than under every table.
